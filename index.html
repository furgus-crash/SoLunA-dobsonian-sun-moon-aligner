📖 User Operation Guide

1. Hardware Setup: Mount your WitMotion WT901BLE67 sensor firmly on the upper tube assembly (near the focuser) of your Dobsonian telescope.

2. Bluetooth Pairing: Power on the sensor, tap Connect BLE, and select your sensor in the device list.

3. Target Selection: Choose ☀️ Sun or 🌙 Moon using the top toggle.

4. Optical Calibration: Point your telescope directly at the target using a solar finder or shadow projection, center it precisely in the eyepiece, and tap Sync / Zero. This records where the target really is and what the sensor reads at that moment. From then on the app follows the target's movement across the sky for you. Sync again any time you re-center it by eye.

5. Field of View: Enter your eyepiece's true field of view in degrees (apparent field of view divided by magnification). The status changes to "Realign Soon" when the target has drifted about 60% of the way to the edge of the view, and to "REALIGN NOW" at about 85%.

6. Handheld Guidance: Hold your phone upright facing the target. Follow the on-screen directional arrow and text prompts to nudge the front opening of the scope. When locked (within 30% of the field radius, and never more than 0.5°), the indicator turns solid and chimes.
