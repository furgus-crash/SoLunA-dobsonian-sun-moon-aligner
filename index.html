📖 User Operation Guide

1. Hardware Setup: Mount your WitMotion WT9011DCL-BT50 sensor firmly on the upper tube assembly (near the focuser) of your Dobsonian telescope.

2. Bluetooth Pairing: Power on the sensor (green light flashing), tap Connect BLE, and select your sensor in the device list.

3. Target Selection: Choose ☀️ Sun or 🌙 Moon using the top toggle.

4. Optical Calibration: Point your telescope directly at the target using a solar finder or shadow projection, center it precisely in the eyepiece, and tap Sync / Zero.

5. Handheld Guidance: Hold your phone upright facing the target. Follow the on-screen directional arrow and text prompts to nudge the front opening of the scope. When locked ($\le 0.5^\circ$), the indicator turns solid and chimes!
