Handheld Phone & Telescope Alignment Guide

Important: Handheld Phone Setup

The phone stays in your hand while the WitMotion WT9011DCL BLE sensor is mounted to the upper optical tube assembly near the eyepiece.

1 Physical Operator Position

Stand directly behind or beside the telescope, facing the direction the telescope tube is pointed (towards the general vicinity of the Sun or Moon).

2 Holding the Phone

Hold the phone upright in front of you naturally. Because you are facing the target, the top of the phone screen points toward the zenith sky and the sides match your physical left and right.

3 Interpreting On-Screen Directions

  • UP / DOWN: Physically tilt the front opening of the telescope tube higher towards the sky (Up) or lower towards the ground (Down).
  • LEFT / RIGHT: Push the front opening of the scope tube to your physical left or right as you face the target.
  • Vector Arrow: Push the front opening in the exact direction the arrow points until the bubble locks in the crosshair.

4 Solar & Lunar Safety

NEVER look directly at the Sun through an unfiltered telescope or finder scope. SoLunA allows you to acquire solar lock completely blind without ever putting your eye near an unshielded eyepiece.
